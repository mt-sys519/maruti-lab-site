## Game and implementation

- The game uses standard Euclidean relationships: triangle interior angles and supplementary angles on a straight line.
- Difficulty is an original presentation structure based on one, two, or three connected triangles and increasing deduction steps.
- Problem generation, scoring, canvas rendering, interface, copy, and visual styling were implemented specifically for MarutiBit. No third-party game assets, question diagrams, audio, or source code are included.
- The initial public patent search found geometry teaching aids and physical triangle puzzles, but no directly matching claim was established by that limited search. This does not rule out relevant patents or designs.
